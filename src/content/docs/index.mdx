## FAQ
<details>
<summary>Why is LightBorn ?</summary>

LightBorn was born from a passion in **E**mergency **L**ightning **S**ystem *(ELS)* developement and a will to fix a long living issue.

Roblox never had a *"standard"* on ELS developement, most of the ELS Systems are either **locked under a paywall, gatekept, incredibly hard to work on or unoptimized.**

From here, LightBorn was created to try and chance that and have a trully **Free and Open Source** standard for ELS developement.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is LightBorn stable ?</summary>

__Not quite yet, but it's getting there.__

LightBorn is currently in **beta** and haven't been used in production yet.

However, it has been proven to hold on smaller projects I have taken parts on.

We are always improving !
</details>

<details>
<summary>How can I trust LightBorn ?</summary>

LightBorn is built dirrectly from source thanks to **GitHub Actions** with **immutable releases**.

If you have a doubt about LightBorn, you can always audit it yourself !
</details>
